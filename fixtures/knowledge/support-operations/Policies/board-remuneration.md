## Committee Composition

The remuneration committee consists of three non-executive directors, one of
whom chairs it. No executive director sits on the committee, and no member
participates in a decision concerning their own remuneration. The committee
meets at least twice each financial year and reports its decisions to the board.

## Disclosure

The committee's decisions are disclosed in the annual report in the form
required by the listing rules. Individual figures are disclosed for executive
directors only. The framework itself is reviewed every three years.
