# Refunds and Chargebacks

This document sets out the authority limits, timings, and record-keeping rules
for refunds, goodwill credits, and chargeback responses. It applies to every
agent in the billing team and to team leaders exercising approval authority.

## Refund Authority Limits

An agent may issue a refund up to and including five hundred euros without
further approval, provided the request is within ninety days of the original
charge and the account holds no open dispute. A refund above five hundred euros
requires a team leader's approval, recorded in the ticket before the refund is
issued.

A refund above five thousand euros requires the billing manager's approval in
addition to the team leader's. The two approvals must come from different
people; an approver may not approve their own request, and a team leader who
raised the request may not act as its approver.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Where the original method is
closed, the agent requests written bank details from the account's registered
contact and escalates to the billing manager, because a refund to a new
destination is the highest-risk payment the team makes.

## Goodwill Credits

A goodwill credit acknowledges service failure without disputing the charge
itself. An agent may apply a goodwill credit up to fifty euros per customer per
calendar year without approval. Beyond that cumulative limit, a team leader
approves each credit individually.

A goodwill credit is never offered before the underlying incident is resolved,
and never in exchange for withdrawing a complaint. The ticket records the
incident reference that justifies the credit; a credit with no incident
reference is reversed at the monthly review.

## Chargeback Response

A chargeback must be answered within seven calendar days of notification from
the payment provider. The response package contains the signed order, the
service activation record, the usage evidence for the disputed period, and the
customer correspondence history.

An agent never contacts the customer about a chargeback directly. All contact
runs through the billing manager, because a customer statement made during a
live dispute becomes evidence the provider will weigh.

Where the evidence package is incomplete, the agent submits what exists before
the deadline rather than missing the deadline while assembling more. A partial
response is weighed; a late response is dismissed.

## Records

Every refund, credit, and chargeback response is recorded against the account
with the amount, the authority relied on, the approver where one was required,
and the reason. The monthly review samples ten per cent of all refunds above
five hundred euros and every refund issued to a payment method other than the
original.
